Where water damage usually starts in a family home

Many water losses begin in the kitchen and laundry area. A dishwasher supply line, a refrigerator ice maker line or a washing machine hose can leak for a long time behind a cabinet or appliance. By the time you see a puddle, the subfloor may already be wet.

We pull wet material only where readings show it is needed, then dry the rest in place. Cabinet toe kicks are often removed so air can move into the base, a step the published ANSI/IICRC S500 industry standard describes for drying wet cabinetry. Ask us what is being removed and why before any demolition starts.

If water is on the floor right now

Stop the flow if safe

Turn off the valve behind the appliance or under the sink. If you cannot reach it safely, shut off the main water supply.

Unplug nothing in standing water

Do not touch plugs, cords or outlets while standing in water. If wet electrical items concern you, call your electric utility or an electrician.

Protect what you can lift

Move rugs, boxes and small furniture to dry ground. Put foil or wood blocks under furniture legs that must stay on wet carpet.

Take photos and call

Photograph the source, the water and the damage, then call and tell us your street.

Contents and belongings

Wet belongings can often be handled in stages. Hard goods like glass and metal can be dried and cleaned. Porous items like upholstery, mattresses and particle board furniture are harder to save. We do not promise that every item can be saved, and we explain what we see before anything is discarded.

If your insurer asks for an inventory, photograph items before you move them and note what was in each room. Your policy and your insurer decide what is covered, and we make no promise about claim approval.

Heavy rain, sewage and when to wait

If water came from a sewer backup or from outside, do not clean it yourself with household fans or an ordinary vacuum. That water can carry contamination. See sewage backup cleanup for how that work differs from a clean water leak.

How do you decide what to remove after a kitchen leak?

We take moisture readings on cabinets, walls and the floor. Material is removed only where readings and condition show it cannot dry in place, and we explain each step.

Can wet carpet and padding be saved?

Sometimes. Clean water caught quickly gives the best chance, and the pad is often replaced. We check before deciding and do not promise that all materials can be saved.
