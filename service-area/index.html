Valrico, the home base

Valrico is a Census-designated place in Hillsborough County, not an incorporated city. The 2020 Census counted 37,895 residents across about 13.81 square miles. It is served by ZIP codes 33594 and 33596, and 33595 is a post office box ZIP.

Neighbors include Brandon to the west, Bloomingdale to the southwest and FishHawk to the south. State Road 60 is the main road to Tampa, and our office sits on East Brandon Boulevard. Most Valrico homes are owner-occupied, so our work centers on clear explanations and documentation.

Utilities and permits across the area

Hillsborough County Water Resources provides drinking water and wastewater for unincorporated areas around Valrico, with a call center at (813) 307-1000. Hillsborough County Development Services handles permits through the HillsGovHub portal.

In Tampa, the City of Tampa Water Department runs the public system and City of Tampa Construction Services issues permits. Which agency applies depends on your address, so confirm before you plan repairs.

  • We tell you what we can do and when, based on conditions at the time.

We do not promise a fixed arrival time, because traffic, storms and demand change it. We tell you honestly what to expect each time you call.

Why the town matters for a water job

Each community around Valrico has its own mix of housing ages, drainage and utilities, and those details shape what a water job looks like. Older homes tend to have older plumbing and finishes, newer subdivisions lean on slab construction, and lower lots can see standing water after heavy rain.

Knowing the area helps dispatch ask better questions on the first call. We ask about the type of home, where the water entered and how long it has been there, and those answers decide which equipment goes on the truck.

Wet season in Hillsborough County runs from late May into early October according to the National Weather Service in Tampa Bay, so storm related calls cluster in those months. Plumbing and appliance failures happen year round, and a cold snap can add frozen pipes in winter.

Before you call

Have the street address and nearest cross street ready, and note whether the water source has been stopped. If it is safe, turn off the water at the main shutoff and keep people away from wet electrical items. Photograph the damage before you move anything.
