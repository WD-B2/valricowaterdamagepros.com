Why Durant sits close to the water

Public records put Durant at about 43 feet above sea level, which is far lower than Valrico at about 102 feet. Edward Medard Park and Reservoir sits just north of the community, east of Turkey Creek Road and south of State Road 60.

On October 11, 2024, the Southwest Florida Water Management District reported that Medard Reservoir rose 6.5 feet after nearly 7 inches of rain. The district released water to the Alafia River, an extra 60 cubic feet per second, which was under 1 percent of the river flow of 11,300 cfs. The Alafia at Lithia Pinecrest Road was at major flood stage and expected to crest.

If your Durant property is close to the reservoir or the river, check the FEMA flood map for your address and ask Hillsborough County Development Services about permits before rebuilding.

A short history that explains the housing mix

The Durant post office opened in 1902, and a rail line now known as the Sarasota Subdivision has run through the area since 1895. CSX acquired it in 1981. Durant High School opened in August 1995, and an F1 tornado struck the community on January 22, 1973.

That mix of older homes, newer subdivisions and storm exposure means no two Durant jobs look alike. Older construction may have different plumbing and wall materials, and our assessment starts with meters rather than assumptions.
