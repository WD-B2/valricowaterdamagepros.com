Who handles water in Tampa

The City of Tampa Water Department serves Tampa and parts of unincorporated Hillsborough County, and it draws from the Hillsborough River. The utilities call center is (813) 274-8811 on weekdays from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. After hours, weekends and holidays, the number is (813) 274-7400.

The City of Tampa Construction Services office issues building permits. If a repair goes beyond cosmetic work, ask them what applies to your address.

Storm surge and Tampa Bay

Tampa sits on the bay, so storm surge is part of the local picture. The National Weather Service summary of Hurricane Helene on September 25 to 27, 2024 recorded 6.83 feet above mean higher high water at the Old Port Tampa tide gauge, and cautioned that point observations may not capture peaks.

Storm surge brings contaminated water into buildings, and our flood damage restoration page explains how those jobs differ from plumbing leaks.
