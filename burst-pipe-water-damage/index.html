Finding the main shutoff before you need it

Most homes have a shutoff valve where the supply line enters the house, often near the water heater, in a garage or at an exterior wall. Many also have a meter box near the street with a curb valve that a wrench can turn. Knowing both locations saves minutes when a joint lets go.

Hillsborough County Public Utilities serves drinking water in unincorporated areas around Valrico, and its call center is (813) 307-1000 if you cannot find or operate the shutoff. If the line that failed is on the utility side of the meter, they are the right call.

What a burst pipe does to a house

Supply lines run at household pressure, so a split pipe keeps pushing water until the valve closes. Water runs down inside walls, soaks insulation, and drips through ceilings into the floor below. A leak inside a wall can run for hours before a stain shows.

Inside walls

Water follows framing down to the bottom plate and can swell drywall from the inside.

Through ceilings

Water pools above a ceiling, then drops in sheets or soaks insulation before it shows.

Under floors

Water slips beneath flooring and into the subfloor where it is hard to see.

Into insulation

Wet insulation holds water against framing and drywall for days.

How we work a burst pipe job

Confirm the shutoff

We check the water is off and note where the failure happened.

Extract the water

Pumps and extractors remove standing water from floors and carpet.

Open and dry

We open walls or ceilings where moisture meters call for it and set air movers and dehumidifiers.

Document and monitor

Photos and daily readings show progress and support your claim.

Once the water is out, structural drying brings the framing and drywall back to normal moisture levels. Where a ceiling has come down or drywall has to be cut, drywall repair puts the room back together.

Common causes in Florida homes

  • Aging supply lines and corroded connections.
  • Failed washing machine hoses and ice maker lines.
  • Water heater connections that crack or loosen.
  • Fittings that fail where a past repair was rushed.
  • Pipes in attics or garages that heat up and expand.
  • Damage from a nail, screw or renovation work.

Frozen lines are less common here but they do happen. See frozen pipe water damage for what a hard freeze does in Hillsborough County.

Burst pipe questions from Valrico homeowners

Do you handle burst pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Valrico?

Yes. We answer 24 hours a day, every day. Call after you shut off the main and dispatch will arrange the first visit as soon as a technician is available.

Do you repair the pipe?

No. A licensed plumber repairs the pipe. We remove the water, dry the structure and document the damage, and we can work around the plumber visit.

What should I do while I wait?

Shut off the main, open a low faucet to drain the line, move belongings out of the wet area and photograph everything. Switch off power to wet rooms only if you can reach the panel safely.

Will my insurance cover a burst pipe?

Many homeowners policies cover sudden pipe failures, but terms differ. Read your policy and talk to your insurer. We document the loss so your claim has the records it needs.

Will I know the cost before work starts?

Yes. We walk through the scope and how the price is set before work starts, and you decide before we begin.
