Water in a crawl space does not stay there. It humidifies the air under the floor, soaks insulation and wood framing, and can push moisture into rooms above. Call our 24/7 line if you see standing water or smell damp earth. We pump out the water, dry the space and check what else it reached.

What a wet crawl space does to a house

Wood framing absorbs moisture slowly. Joists, subfloor and sill plates can stay damp for weeks after a rain event if ventilation is poor. Wet insulation sags and holds water against the floor above.

Ductwork and plumbing under the house are also exposed. Wet ducts can sweat, and fittings can corrode over time.

Why crawl spaces take on water

Rain and runoff

Heavy rain can send water across the lot and through foundation vents.

Plumbing leaks

A drain or supply line leak under the floor drips into soil and insulation.

Air conditioning

Condensation from ducts and air handlers can drip into the space.

High water table

Wet soil keeps the crawl space damp even without a visible leak.

The National Weather Service in Tampa Bay lists the wet season as May 25 through October 10. If your crawl space holds water, that stretch is when it is most likely to show up.

How we dry a crawl space

Inspect safely

We check access, electrical hazards and the extent of standing water.

Pump out

Submersible pumps remove standing water from low spots.

Remove soaked material

Wet insulation and vapor barrier sections are removed where they cannot dry.

Dry and monitor

Dehumidifiers and air movers run with readings on framing and subfloor.

If the leak came from a supply line or drain, see burst pipe water damage or sewage backup cleanup. After a storm, storm water damage cleanup covers the wider picture.

Signs the space needs attention

  • A musty smell inside the house that is strongest near floors.
  • Floors that feel soft, bouncy or cupped.
  • Insulation hanging down or dark with moisture.
  • Condensation on ducts or pipes.
  • Standing water or mud after rain.
  • Pest activity that follows damp conditions.

Crawl space questions from Valrico homeowners

Do you treat crawl space water as an emergency?

If water is deep or coming from a leak, yes. Our 24/7 line is answered every day including nights and weekends, so call when you find it.

Can wet insulation be saved?

Usually not. Fiberglass that stays wet loses its value and holds water against wood, so we remove it and replace it after drying.

Do you fix drainage outside the house?

No. A drainage or landscaping contractor handles grading and downspouts. We remove the water and dry the space.

How long does a crawl space take to dry?

A few days to a couple of weeks depending on how wet the wood is and how much air can move through the space.

Will I know the cost before work starts?

Yes. We explain the scope and how the price is set before work starts, and you decide before we begin.
