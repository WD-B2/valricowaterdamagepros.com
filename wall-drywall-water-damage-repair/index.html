Drywall absorbs water and loses strength. A stain, bubbling paint or a soft spot in a wall tells you moisture is in the cavity behind it. Call our 24/7 line. We locate the water with meters, dry the wall or cut out what cannot be saved, and finish the repair so it matches the room.

How water gets into walls

Water enters walls from above, from inside and from below. A roof leak runs down framing, a pipe leak wets the cavity directly and floor water wicks up from the base.

From a floor leak

Baseboards and wall bottoms wick water upward by several inches.

From a pipe in the wall

Supply and drain lines leak inside the cavity and run down.

From the ceiling

Water travels along framing and wets walls below.

From windows and doors

Failed seals let rain into wall cavities around openings.

Dry it or cut it out

Not every wet wall has to come out. Clean-water leaks caught early can often be dried through small holes with air movers and dehumidifiers. Drywall that has swelled, crumbled or been soaked by floodwater or sewage is cut out and replaced.

Insulation in the cavity is also removed when it is wet because it holds water against the framing.

Find moisture

Meters and thermal imaging show how far water has traveled.

Open the wall

We remove baseboards and cut a clean line above the wet area when needed.

Dry the cavity

Air movers and dehumidifiers run until framing and remaining drywall meet target readings.

Rebuild

New drywall is hung, taped, textured, primed and painted.

Matching the finish

A good repair disappears into the wall. We match the existing texture, whether it is smooth, orange peel or knockdown, and prime and paint so the patch blends.

Where a whole wall has been affected, we often paint the full wall to the corners so the color reads evenly.

  • Remove baseboards and trim carefully so they can be reused.
  • Cut at a stud line for a clean patch.
  • Treat framing and replace insulation.
  • Tape, mud and sand in thin coats.
  • Texture to match the surrounding wall.
  • Prime and paint corner to corner.

Drywall water damage questions from Valrico homeowners

Can I just paint over a water stain?

Not until the wall is dry and the source is fixed. Paint over damp drywall peels, traps moisture and often brings the stain back.

Does wet drywall always have to be replaced?

No. Clean-water leaks caught early can often be dried in place. Swollen, crumbly or contaminated drywall has to go.

How long does drywall repair take?

Drying takes days, and the patch, texture and paint take a few more because of drying time between coats.

Do you match texture and paint?

Yes. We match the existing texture and finish and paint the repaired wall so it blends.

Yes. Our line is answered 24 hours a day, every day.
