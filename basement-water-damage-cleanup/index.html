What counts as a basement in Florida

Full basements are uncommon here because the water table is high, and most Valrico homes sit on slabs or raised foundations. This page covers the spaces that behave like basements, including walkout levels, sunken rooms, below-grade garages and lower floors where grade rises behind the walls.

Whatever you call the space, below-grade rooms collect water. Gravity pulls it in from the lot, and it has nowhere to go except pumps.

How lower levels flood

Rain and runoff

Heavy rain pushes water against walls and doors at the lowest level.

Plumbing failures

A supply line or drain leak fills the lowest room first.

Sump or drain failures

Pumps and drains stop working during power loss or heavy rain.

Rising groundwater

Soil saturated by days of rain pushes water through floor joints and walls.

How we clear a lower level

Make it safe

Power is checked before anyone enters and hazards are noted.

Pump out

Pumps drain standing water in stages to avoid stressing walls and floors.

Remove and clean

Soaked carpet, padding and drywall come out, and hard surfaces are cleaned.

Dry and monitor

Dehumidifiers and air movers run with readings until the space is dry.

Below-grade spaces dry slowly, so structural drying often takes longer than in upper rooms. If rain caused the water, storm water damage cleanup covers the wider picture. For carpet down there, see carpet water damage restoration.

After the water is gone

  • Dry every surface and cavity before closing walls.
  • Check for damp insulation and soft framing.
  • Review your drainage, gutters and downspouts.
  • Consider a backflow valve if drains have backed up.
  • Ask whether sump pumps and battery backups are working.

Sump pumps and drains in lower spaces

If your lower level has a sump pump or a floor drain, check that it is running and that the discharge line is clear. Pumps fail in storms when power goes out, so a battery backup is worth asking about. A plumber or drainage contractor can service the system while we remove the water that already got in.

Lower level water questions from Valrico homeowners

Do you handle basement and lower level flooding at night or on weekends in Valrico?

Yes. Our line is answered 24 hours a day, every day, including holidays and during storms.

Should I pump the water out myself?

A small amount can be handled with a sump pump, but deep water should be removed gradually and only after power is confirmed safe. Call us for anything beyond a shallow puddle.

Will the walls need to be removed?

Only the materials that cannot dry, typically wet drywall and insulation. Concrete and block usually dry in place.

Can this happen again?

If the cause is drainage, yes. A drainage or plumbing contractor can fix the source so the lower level stays dry.
