Mold grows where moisture lingers, and Valrico humidity gives it plenty of chances. If you see dark spots, smell something musty or know a leak was never fully dried, call our 24/7 line. We find the moisture, contain the area and remove what has grown.

Musty smell, dark spots or a leak that never fully dried: could it be mold?

Those signs often show up together. A smell that gets stronger when the air conditioner is off, dark or fuzzy patches on drywall or ceilings, and paint that peels near a past leak all suggest damp material. Only testing or a trained inspection can confirm what a patch is.

We do not make health claims about mold. If you or someone in the home has health concerns, talk to a physician.

Moisture control comes first

Removing mold without fixing the moisture only buys time. A leaking pipe, a roof leak or a humid air handler will bring it back. We pair mold work with structural drying and a plumber, roofer or HVAC technician when needed.

How remediation works

Mold remediation follows the ANSI/IICRC S520 standard as an industry guide. The goal is to contain the affected area, remove contaminated material and clean surfaces so spores are not spread through the house.

Assess

Moisture readings and a visual review show where mold is and how far it goes.

Contain

Plastic barriers and negative air keep spores from moving to clean rooms.

Remove and clean

Affected porous materials are removed, and surfaces are HEPA vacuumed and cleaned.

Dry and rebuild

The area is dried to target readings before drywall and finishes are replaced.

Testing, inspection and clearance for a home sale or loan

Buyers, lenders and insurers sometimes ask for a mold inspection or a clearance report. A clearance check is done after remediation to confirm the work area is clean. We cannot promise that a lender, buyer or insurer will accept a report. They set their own requirements.

Florida licenses mold assessors and mold remediators as individuals, not businesses. A company that does both needs a separate license for each service, according to the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation. Under state law, an assessor may not remediate a structure that the same assessor or company assessed in the previous twelve months, and the reverse applies to remediators.

Ask who will do the work, ask for their license and keep the inspection and the remediation separate. For help with the questions to ask, see your lender or insurer first.

Mold questions from Valrico homeowners

Musty smell but no visible mold: what should I do?

Moisture may be hiding in walls, ceilings or under floors. A moisture check and an inspection can find it before growth spreads. Call our 24/7 line and we can start with readings.

Does bleach kill mold?

Bleach can lighten surface stains but does not fix the moisture or remove growth in porous materials. Porous materials often have to be removed.

Do I need a mold test?

Not always. Visible growth and a known water event are often enough to start. A buyer, lender or insurer may require testing.

Can you tell me about clearance for a sale or loan?

Clearance is a post-remediation check by an assessor. Ask the lender what they require, and keep the assessor separate from the remediator as state rules require.
