How a freeze turns into a flood

Water expands as it freezes. Ice inside a pipe can split the wall of the pipe or push a fitting apart, though nothing leaks while the ice holds. When temperatures rise, the ice melts and water pours from the crack at full line pressure.

That delay fools people. A cold morning ends, the house warms up and the first sign of trouble is a stain on a ceiling or a puddle in a hallway.

Freezes do reach Hillsborough County

Florida homes often have pipes in attics, garages, exterior walls and crawl spaces with little insulation, because a hard freeze is rare. The National Weather Service Tampa Bay summary of the 2022 Christmas Eve and Christmas Day cold ranks that event among the three coldest on record for the area.

If you are prepping ahead of a forecast freeze, a few steps help. Wrap exposed pipes, open cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls, let a faucet drip, and know where the main shutoff is.

Attic and garage lines

Uninsulated supply lines in unheated spaces are the first to freeze.

Exterior wall plumbing

Pipes in outside walls lose heat quickly on a cold night.

Outdoor spigots and irrigation

Hose bibs and irrigation backflow lines are exposed to the air.

Water heater connections

Cold garages can chill the lines feeding and leaving a tank heater.

Yes. We answer 24 hours a day, every day. Freezes often hit overnight, so the line is open when you need it.

Should I thaw a frozen pipe myself?

If no water is leaking, a hair dryer on low or warm towels can thaw a pipe gently. Never use an open flame, and stop if you see a crack or hear water. Call a plumber and us if the pipe has split.

Can a pipe burst even if it does not freeze solid?

Yes. Ice forming in one section builds pressure in the rest of the line, and the split can happen in a different place than the ice.

Do you repair the pipe?
