Hot floors, a spinning meter or damp carpet: is it a slab leak?

Those symptoms often send homeowners searching for answers before they ever hear the term slab leak. A floor that feels warm underfoot, carpet that stays damp with no spill, or a water bill that jumps with no change in habits can all trace back to a pipe in or under the concrete.

Many Florida homes sit on a concrete slab with pipes running through or beneath it. A leak in one of those lines pushes water up through cracks and joints or under flooring. Because the pipe is buried, the damage can build for a long time.

Signs of a leak under the slab

  • A water meter that spins when every fixture is off.
  • A hot spot on the floor.
  • The sound of running water in quiet rooms.
  • Baseboards or flooring that swell and lift.
  • Musty smells in rooms with no obvious source.
  • Unexplained spikes in the water bill.

A plumber confirms the leak with listening gear or cameras. Once the leak is located, we can start drying the surrounding area.

What water does on a slab home

Water that rises through the slab spreads under flooring and into wall bottoms. It wicks into baseboards, cabinet bases and drywall. Tile may hide the leak while grout lines carry water outward.

Under flooring

Wood and laminate cup and lift when water sits below them.

Behind baseboards

Drywall wicks water up from the floor plate.

Under cabinets

Cabinet bases absorb water and can swell or delaminate.

Across rooms

Water follows low spots and doorways into neighboring rooms.

Slab leak questions from Valrico homeowners

Do you handle slab leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Valrico?

Yes. Our line is answered 24 hours a day, every day. If water is rising through the floor at night, call and dispatch will help right away.

Why is my floor warm or damp when I cannot see a leak?

A hot water line under the slab can warm the floor above it, and a cold line can leave a damp patch that never dries. A plumber can confirm with a pressure test or listening gear, and we can dry the affected floor while the leak is traced.

Do you find and fix the leak?

No. A plumber locates and repairs the leak. We remove the water and dry the floors and walls.

Will the leak damage the foundation?

It can in rare cases when water erodes soil under a slab, but most slab leak damage is to flooring, cabinets and walls. A plumber and a structural professional can advise if you see cracks.

How long before flooring is safe to replace?

Only after readings show the subfloor and slab are dry. Replacing flooring too early traps moisture and leads to cupping or mold.

Yes. We explain the scope and how the price is set before work starts, and you decide before we begin.
