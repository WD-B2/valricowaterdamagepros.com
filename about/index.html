Who we are

Valrico Water Damage Pros is a water damage restoration service based at 1901 E Brandon Blvd in Valrico, Florida. We answer one phone line around the clock, and when you call we send a technician to remove water, dry the structure and document the loss.

We serve Valrico and the nearby communities of Durant, Dover, Lithia and Tampa. Our work covers everything from water extraction to drywall repair, and we treat mold remediation as a secondary service that follows moisture control.

How a job runs from our side

You call

Dispatch asks where you are, what you see and whether the water source is stopped, then talks through safe first steps.

We assess

A technician maps moisture with meters and thermal imaging and explains what was found and what is recommended.

You decide

We explain the scope and how the price is set before work starts, and you decide before any equipment goes down.

We work and document

Water is removed, equipment dries the structure and daily readings and photos are kept for you and your insurer.

What we measure and why

Water damage looks finished long before it is. Surfaces can feel dry while framing and subfloors are still wet, and that hidden moisture is how mold and odors start. For that reason we measure instead of guessing.

The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard is the published industry reference for water damage restoration, and we use it as a guide for how drying is measured and documented. The S520 standard serves the same role for mold. Those are statements about industry standards and not certification claims by this business.

What we do not do

  • We do not repair pipes, roofs or air conditioners. A plumber, roofer or HVAC technician does that, and we work around those visits.
  • We do not make health claims about mold. If you have health concerns, talk to a physician.
  • We do not promise arrival times. We tell you honestly when we can be there once we know your location and the conditions.
  • We do not tell you what your insurance policy covers. Your insurer decides that.
  • We do not use forms. You call, and a person answers.

Licensing and insurance

We are licensed and insured. Florida licenses mold assessors and mold remediators as individuals, not businesses, so for mold work it is worth asking who will do the work and what license that person holds. An assessor may not remediate a structure that the same assessor or company assessed within the previous 12 months, according to the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation.

The Valrico we work in

Valrico is a Census-designated place in Hillsborough County and is not an incorporated city. The 2020 Census counted 37,895 residents across about 13.81 square miles, and Census QuickFacts lists a 79.8 percent owner-occupied housing rate and a median home value of $356,200 for 2019 to 2023.

Most Valrico homes are owner-occupied, which is why we put so much weight on documentation and clear scope. Homeowners deserve to know what is being done and why, especially during a stressful loss. Hillsborough County Water Resources provides drinking water and wastewater in unincorporated areas, and Hillsborough County Development Services handles permits.

How we communicate during a job

You should never have to guess what is happening in your home. Before equipment goes down we explain what we found, what we recommend and how the price is set. As work continues we share moisture readings and photos so you can see the drying progress for yourself.

If conditions change, for example if drying shows water traveled further than first visible, we tell you right away and explain how the scope changes. You decide before anything beyond the original plan goes ahead.

When the job is done we leave you with a clear record of what was found and what was done. Those records are useful for an insurance claim, for a future sale of the home and for your own peace of mind.
