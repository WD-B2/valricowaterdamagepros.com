How storms damage homes with water

Roof and attic

Wind lifts shingles and flashing, letting rain soak insulation and drip through ceilings.

Windows and doors

Wind-driven rain pushes water past seals and frames.

Low spots and entries

Heavy rain pools and flows through garage doors and thresholds.

Rising water

Flooding from rivers, drainage and surge enters at ground level.

Hillsborough County permit note

Cosmetic repairs such as drywall replacement do not need a permit in unincorporated Hillsborough County, but the county asks for a Substantial Damage Packet. Rebuilding or structural repair in a Special Flood Hazard Area needs a permit.

Storm water damage questions from Valrico homeowners

Do you handle storm water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Valrico?

Yes. We answer 24 hours a day, every day, including during storm season. After a large storm, demand can be high, so we triage by urgency and safety.

Do you tarp roofs?

No. A roofer handles tarping and roof repair. We handle interior water removal and drying.

Is storm damage covered by insurance?

Wind and wind-driven rain are often covered by homeowners policies, but flood is usually separate. Read your policy and talk to your insurer.

What should I photograph?

Photograph the water, the damaged areas, the source and any items you move. Video can help show scale.
