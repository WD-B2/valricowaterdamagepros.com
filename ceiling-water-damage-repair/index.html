Why a wet ceiling is urgent

Drywall absorbs water and loses strength. A ceiling with a pool of water above it can bring down wet drywall, insulation and light fixtures all at once. Wet insulation also holds moisture against framing long after the leak stops.

Never turn on a ceiling fan or light if water is dripping through the fixture. Turn off the breaker for that room only if you can reach the panel without standing in water.

Finding where the water comes from

The stain on the ceiling is rarely directly under the source. Water travels along joists and pipes before it finds a place to drip.

Plumbing above

Bathrooms, laundry rooms and water heaters on an upper floor can leak into the ceiling below.

Air conditioning

Overflowing drain pans and blocked condensate lines are common in humid climates.

Roof or attic

Rain or flashing failures can saturate attic insulation before the ceiling shows it.

Condensation

Poorly insulated ducts sweat and drip into the ceiling in warm humid weather.

If the source is an air handler, see AC leak water damage. If it is a supply line above, burst pipe water damage explains how those jobs run.

Roof leaks that reach the ceiling

Many ceiling stains start at the roof. A roofer repairs the roof, but the interior damage still needs attention. Roof leak water often soaks attic insulation first, then travels across the ceiling and drips weeks later.

After a storm, look for stains that spread after rain, bubbling paint and musty smells in the attic. Tarping and shingle repair belong to a roofer. We remove wet insulation and ceiling drywall, dry the framing and rebuild. For storm-driven leaks, see storm water damage cleanup.

Valrico homeowners should know the wet season runs May 25 through October 10 per the National Weather Service in Tampa Bay. Leaks tend to show up when rain events stack up, so check ceilings after a heavy week.

Ceiling water damage questions from Valrico homeowners

Do you handle ceiling water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Valrico?

Yes. Our line is answered 24 hours a day, every day. A sagging ceiling is a safety risk, so call as soon as you see it and dispatch will help right away.

Can a stained ceiling be painted over?

Only after the source is fixed and the material is dry. Painting over a damp ceiling traps moisture and the stain will come back.

Do you repair the roof?

No. A roofer repairs the roof. We remove wet interior materials, dry the structure and rebuild the ceiling after the roof is fixed.

How do I know if the leak is from the roof or plumbing?

Stains that grow after rain point toward the roof, while stains that show up without rain point toward plumbing or air conditioning. A moisture meter and a look in the attic usually settle it.
