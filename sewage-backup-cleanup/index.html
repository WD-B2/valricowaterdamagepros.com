Gurgling drains, a sewage smell, or water coming up in a tub or floor drain all point to a backup. Stop using water in the house, keep children and pets out of the area, and call our 24/7 line. Do not try to clean it with bare hands or a household mop.

Gurgling drains, bad smells, water in the tub: what is going on?

Those symptoms usually mean a blockage or a failure in the line that carries waste out of the house. Water that cannot drain finds the lowest opening, which is often a shower, tub or floor drain.

A plumber finds and clears the blockage. Until that happens, any water you run in the house can add to the problem.

Why sewage is handled differently

Sewage contains bacteria and other contaminants. Industry standards classify it as the most contaminated category of water, so porous materials such as carpet, padding and drywall that it touches are generally removed rather than dried in place.

We wear protective equipment, contain the work area and discard affected materials properly. Hard surfaces and framing are cleaned and sanitized.

Carpet and padding

Who handles which part

Hillsborough County Water Resources serves wastewater in unincorporated areas and has a call center at (813) 307-1000. If the blockage is in the county main, they handle it. If it is in your private line, you need a plumber. The Florida Department of Health in Hillsborough County oversees septic systems for homes that are not on sewer.

The county also runs a Septic and Low Pressure Sewer Conversion Initiative, so ask which category your address falls into.

Sewage backup questions from Valrico homeowners

Do you handle sewage backup emergencies at night or on weekends in Valrico?

Yes. We answer 24 hours a day, every day. A backup does not wait for business hours, so call as soon as you notice it.

Why are my drains gurgling and is that a sign of a backup?

Gurgling often means air is trapped by a blockage in the line. It can come before a backup, so stop using water and call a plumber and us if you see water rising.

Can I clean it myself?

Small spills on hard surfaces can be handled with gloves and proper disinfectant, but larger backups need protective gear and proper disposal. Call us for anything beyond a small spill.

Is my carpet ruined?

Carpet and padding touched by sewage are generally removed. We explain what we find before we cut anything.
