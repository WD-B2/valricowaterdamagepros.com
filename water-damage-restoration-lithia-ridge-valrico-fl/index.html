Finding water you cannot see

A stain on a ceiling, a soft spot in a floor or a musty smell can mean water is moving through the structure. We use moisture meters and, where useful, thermal imaging to map how far wet material extends. Mapping first helps us decide what can be dried in place and what must be opened.

Age is not a diagnosis. Homes from the same period can have had floors, appliances and water heaters replaced at different times. That is why we assess each house instead of assuming what is behind the wall.

What to do before a technician arrives

Stop the source when safe

Close the valve to the fixture or shut off the main if the area around it is dry. Do not wade through water to reach a shutoff.

Avoid electrical hazards

Keep away from outlets and cords in wet rooms. Do not operate breakers while standing in water.

Move belongings you can lift safely

Lift small items off wet floors and set them on dry surfaces. Leave heavy furniture for the crew.

Photograph and call

Photograph the source and the damage, then call and give us your street and the time you first noticed water.

Questions from Lithia Ridge homeowners

Which ZIP code does Lithia Ridge use?

Sales listings use 33596 and one data site lists 33594. We do not rely on either. Give dispatch your street address and we will confirm the visit from that.

How do you find hidden water in a larger home?

We use moisture meters on walls, floors and baseboards, then map where readings stay high. Thermal imaging can help show where wet material continues. The map tells us what to dry and what to open.

Does the age of my home mean more water damage?

No. Age alone does not predict damage. Appliances, flooring and plumbing parts are replaced over time, so we measure each house instead of guessing.

Do you repair the leaking pipe too?

No. A plumber repairs pipes, and we remove water, dry the structure and document the damage. We work around the plumber visit so drying starts as soon as the source is stopped.

Can you give a price before you start?

Yes. We explain the scope and how the price is set before work starts, and you decide before equipment goes down.
