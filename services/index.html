What to do while you wait for help

Safe first steps limit the damage. Follow the ones that apply, and call the 24/7 line for the rest. This is general guidance based on the ANSI/IICRC S500 water damage standard as an industry reference.

  • Stop the water at its source if it is safe to do so.
  • Do not enter standing water near electrical outlets or appliances.
  • Turn off power to wet areas only if you can reach the panel safely and dry.
  • Move small valuables and furniture out of the wet area.
  • Photograph the damage before you move or throw anything away.
