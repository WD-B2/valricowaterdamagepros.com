Floodwater is not the same as a plumbing leak

Water from a broken supply line starts clean. Floodwater picks up soil, fuel, garbage and sewage as it moves, so we treat it as contaminated. That changes what can be dried in place and what has to be removed and discarded.

Porous materials like carpet, padding and drywall soaked by floodwater are usually removed. Hard surfaces and framing are cleaned and dried. We explain which category applies before work starts.

Flooding in the Valrico area

Valrico sits near the Alafia River. Not every flooded home sits near a river. Heavy rain can also overwhelm drainage and push water through doors and low spots. To see how your address is classified, check the FEMA flood map and ask Hillsborough County Development Services.

Permits and the 50 percent rule

Cosmetic repairs such as drywall replacement do not require a permit in unincorporated Hillsborough County, but the county still asks for a Substantial Damage Packet. Rebuilding or structural repair in a Special Flood Hazard Area needs a permit, and the 50 percent substantial damage rule can apply there.

Flood-damaged electrical systems must be inspected before power is restored. Hillsborough County Development Services handles permits through the HillsGovHub portal, and the county can tell you what applies to your address.

Waiting for the water to recede

Flood restoration questions from Valrico homeowners

Do you handle flood emergencies at night or on weekends in Valrico?

Yes. We answer 24 hours a day, every day, including during storms. Call and dispatch will take your address and details and send help as soon as conditions allow.

Is it safe to stay in the house?

It depends on how high the water is and whether power is on. Avoid standing water near electrical equipment and leave if the water is rising. Call 911 for any immediate danger.

Can wet carpet be saved after a flood?

Floodwater usually means carpet and padding are removed, because they cannot be reliably cleaned. Hard surfaces can often be cleaned and dried.

How do I know if my home is in a flood zone?

Check the FEMA flood map for your address and ask Hillsborough County Development Services. They can also tell you which permit rules apply.
