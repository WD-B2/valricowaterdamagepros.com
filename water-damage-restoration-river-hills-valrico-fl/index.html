Water damage help for River Hills homeowners

River Hills is a deed-restricted, controlled-access community in south Valrico, next to FishHawk. The golf club sits at 3943 New River Hills Parkway, and the first home was built in 1988 according to the Osprey Observer. The community has grown up around the course and the conservation land at its edge.

When water gets into a River Hills home, the first question is where it came from. A supply line, an air conditioner drain, a water heater or a roof leak each calls for a different response. We start with that answer, then remove water, measure what is still wet and dry the structure.

What we know about the River Hills area

  • The Osprey Observer reports that the Joe Lee course and clubhouse opened in 1989, a year after the first home.
  • A local real estate guide describes the homes as one story and two story single family houses on lots of roughly one fifth to over half an acre.
  • The same guide lists about 1,160 single family homes, and describes a trail system along the Alafia River and conservation land on the eastern side.
  • Sales listings for River Hills streets such as Turkey Oak Drive use ZIP code 33596.

We do not have a verified FEMA flood zone for River Hills as a whole. Neighborhood specific FEMA flood zone status not verified. Flood zones are mapped by property, so check the FEMA Flood Map Service Center with your address.

If water is spreading in your River Hills home

Stop the source if it is safe

Close the shutoff valve for the fixture or appliance. If you cannot find the cause, shut off the main at the street side or meter only when the area around it is dry.

Stay out of standing water near power

Do not touch outlets, cords or breakers while standing in water. If a panel is wet, call your electric utility or an electrician.

Photograph before you move things

Take wide and close photos of the damage and the source. Lift small valuables and papers off wet floors.

Call for a professional assessment

Describe the room, the source and how long water has been present. We will explain what happens next.

Water extraction and drying in a gated community

Large drying equipment has to get to the house, so access matters in a controlled-access community. Tell the gate or management office that a restoration technician is expected, and have your address and a contact number ready. Our dispatcher will tell you what to expect once the visit is arranged, and we do not promise a fixed arrival time.

On site we use moisture meters to find water in drywall, baseboards and subfloors, since surfaces often feel dry while framing is still wet. Air movers and dehumidifiers then run while we log readings each visit. The goal is dry materials, not a dry looking room.

Supply line and appliance leaks

A failed hose behind a dishwasher or washer can wet floors and cabinets. See appliance leak cleanup.

Air conditioner drain overflow

A clogged condensate line can stain ceilings and soak insulation. Read about AC leak water damage.

Water heater failures

A leaking tank can wet a garage or closet and the walls beside it. See water heater leak cleanup.

Wet ceilings and walls

Water that travels along framing may show up far from its source. See ceiling water damage repair.

Insurance records for a River Hills claim

Your policy and your insurer decide what is covered, and coverage depends on the cause of the damage. What we can do is keep a clear record of the work. Photos, moisture readings and a written scope can help describe what was found and what was done.

The Florida Department of Financial Services publishes a consumer claims guide. It says an insurer must acknowledge a claim within 7 days and, in most cases, pay or deny within 60 days. Keep copies of every message with your insurer.

The Alafia River and heavy rain

The Alafia River runs through the Valrico area. In October 2024 Hillsborough County issued flood warnings along the river during Hurricane Milton, and FOX Weather reported that the river in Valrico was swollen. That is county and river level information. It does not say which River Hills homes, if any, were affected.

If rain or river water has entered your home, treat it as contaminated until it is assessed. See our flood damage restoration page and our sewage backup cleanup page for how that work differs from a clean water leak.

Questions from River Hills homeowners

Where is River Hills in Valrico?

River Hills is in south Valrico next to FishHawk. The country club address is 3943 New River Hills Parkway. Give dispatch your own street so the right crew can find the house.

Do you need gate access to reach a River Hills home?

Plan on it. River Hills is described as a controlled-access community, so tell the gate or management office to expect a technician and share your address and phone number.

Can a technician tell if the water is from the Alafia River?

A technician looks at the source, the water color and smell, and where it entered. Water from outside is treated as contaminated, and the work plan changes to match.

What should I photograph before cleanup starts?

Take wide shots of each wet room and close shots of the source, wet materials and any damaged belongings. Photos taken before you move things help describe the loss to your insurer.

Will I know the cost before work starts?

Yes. We explain the scope and how the price is set before work starts, and you decide before equipment goes down.
